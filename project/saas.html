SAAS : Software as a Service

What is SAAS?

SAAS (Software as a Service) is a cloud based security system which works by directly accessing the internert via streaming it to your computer. It is also the most restrictive out of them all as the Vendor is incontrol of security, identity management, and data streaming; while the user only has permissions to use the information only. Just because you only ahve rights to the use of the service, doesn't mean safety is garenteed. It is important to make sure that all personal information is confidential and remains confidential, say for example your passwords and credit card numbers. Think of The SAAS security system like an apartment. You'll have a landlord (the vendor) who is responsible for the building's outer walls, the front gate, and the structural integrity. But you are responsible for locking your apartment door, deciding who you give a key to, and making sure you don't leave your valuables sitting out by an open window.

Pros and Cons

Pros

• Lower Upfront Costs
As a subscription model, SAAS is famous for its relatively low upfront costs. As such, SAAS is very benificial in the short term model because the heavy expenditure that can be taken due to liscencing will heavily inhibit growth.
• High Accessability and Rapid Deployment
Since SAAS systems are largelty stored in the cloud, the accessability of these system is fairly easy as long as you have a connection to an internet source and a web browser. Adding onto the fact that it is widely accessable, the storage of data in the cloud makes it increadibly easy to deploy these systems as most of the data required is already pre-configured and ready to be released once a user signs up. No need for installs or manual updates.
• Automatic Maintainence and scalability
Finally, since this is a SAAS system, the provider is responsible for most of the maintinence such as software updates and security bugs. Alongside that, it's constand maintainence and could access allows for the site to be used at large, making it very easy to scale according to a user's needs.

Cons

• High Internet dependancy
Because SAAS applications live in the cloud, a stable internet connection is required. Connection issues or service outages will restrict access to tools and data, practically making the use of SAAS application virtually impossible.
• Data security concerns
Although SAAS systems make sure to keep the utmost security, there is still the posibility of a breach in the system. Unfortunately unlike other types of systems, you can't really protect yourself from data breaches, making your information highly vunerable to breaches.
• High Costs
Lastly come the costs. Although SAAS systems have low upfront costs, the gradual use of such systems combined with higher subscription fees will lead to you paying more towards these systems than if you had bought the liscence seperately altogether.
